Export function sumLengths(text, unit). text must be a string of 1 to 30 terms separated by '+', with optional ASCII spaces around each term and around '+'. Each term is a nonnegative decimal written as one or more ASCII digits optionally followed by '.' and 1 to 3 digits, immediately followed by 'mm', 'cm', or 'm'. No other whitespace, signs, exponent, or syntax is allowed. Each term's numeric value must be at most 1000000. unit must be 'mm', 'cm', or 'm'. Reject any invalid argument with RangeError. Add the lengths exactly, convert the sum to unit, and return a string with exactly two decimal places, rounding to nearest with exact halfway cases rounded away from zero. No floating-point approximation may change the result. Leading zeros are accepted and are not preserved in output.
