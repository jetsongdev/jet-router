Export function lineWinner(board, k). board must be an array of 1 to 12 rows, each an array of 1 to 12 cells of identical width. Each cell must be exactly null, 'A', or 'B'. k must be an integer from 1 through max(height, width). Otherwise throw RangeError. A player wins if at least k consecutive cells of that player occur horizontally, vertically, or on either diagonal; longer runs count. Return 'A' or 'B' if exactly that player has a winning run, 'both' if both do, and null if neither does. Ignore turn counts and whether the position could arise in play. Validate the entire board before deciding the result. Do not mutate board or its rows.
